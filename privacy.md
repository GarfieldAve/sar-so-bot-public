# SAR-SO Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SAR-SO Bot ("the Bot," "we," or "us") collects, uses, stores, and manages information when users interact with its features.

The Bot is designed for event management, moderation, participation tracking, and administrative recordkeeping within participating Discord communities.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on which features are used, the Bot may process the following information:

**Discord Information**
- Discord user IDs and server IDs.
- Discord usernames, display names, and role information.
- Information submitted through Bot commands, buttons, and forms.
- Event attendance and participation records.

**Roblox Information**
- Roblox usernames and user IDs.
- Associations between Discord and Roblox accounts.
- Information needed to identify participants and maintain records.

**Event and Participation Information**
- Event types, dates, hosts, and attendees.
- Event descriptions and submitted evidence links.
- Participation points and reward records.

**Administrative Information**
- Moderation cases, warnings, and punishments.
- Administrative actions and event edit history.
- Audit records identifying the users who performed recorded actions.

The Bot is not intended to collect passwords, payment card details, or other unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## 3. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

- Identify users and their linked Roblox accounts.
- Create and maintain event participation records.
- Synchronize participation points with Google Sheets.
- Notify users and officers about participation requirements.
- Support moderation and administrative processes.
- Maintain audit records and investigate potential misuse.
- Diagnose technical problems and improve Bot functionality.

We do not use collected information for targeted advertising.

## 4. Data Storage

The Bot uses a database to maintain operational records, including user identifiers, account links, event information, and administrative history.

Some participation information may also be transferred to Google Sheets for authorized recordkeeping and reward management.

Access to stored information is intended to be limited to the Bot's operator, authorized administrators, and relevant third-party services where necessary for the Bot's operation.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Bot may interact with:

- **Discord:** To process commands, identify members, and send notifications.
- **Roblox:** To identify Roblox accounts and associate them with Discord users.
- **Google Sheets:** To maintain participation records and synchronize rewards.

Information transferred to these services is also subject to their applicable privacy policies.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to support the Bot's functions, maintain accurate records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Some event records may be permanently deleted through authorized administrative commands. Separate audit records may be retained to preserve accountability.

Removing an event from the Bot's database does not necessarily remove information already stored in external services or historical backups.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of record and its administrative purpose.

## 7. Data Access and Correction

Users may contact the Bot's operator to request information about their stored data or report inaccurate records.

Requests may include:

- Access to personal information associated with their account.
- Correction of inaccurate Discord or Roblox account associations.
- Removal of eligible personal information.
- Information about how their data is used.

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable laws and legitimate administrative recordkeeping needs.

Certain information may need to be retained for security, accountability, or legal reasons.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no database, online service, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for users who are permitted to use Discord under Discord's age requirements and applicable laws.

The Bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use Discord.

If you believe information has been collected from an ineligible child, contact the Bot's operator.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features, integrations, or data-handling practices change.

The date at the top of this document will indicate the most recent revision.
